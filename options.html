Date and time tokens

Tokens apply only to rules whose type is Date/time. Plain-text output preserves every character.

YYYY
4-digit year
YY
2-digit year
MM
month, 01–12
DD
day, 01–31
HH
hour, 00–23
hh
hour, 01–12
mm
minute, 00–59
ss
second, 00–59
A
AM or PM
\x
literal x — use it when your text contains a token, e.g. co\m\ment
